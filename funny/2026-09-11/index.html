Ep. 1295 - 9/11 And the Search for Accountability

The Andrew Klavan Show · 11 September 2026 · 1h 15m · times link into the video · written up 11 September at 18:31

  1. “The first day, I was so jet-lagged from a trip to England that I forgot to put my shoes on and arrived wearing bedroom slippers. I stumbled through a short opening podcast that ended with that Bible quote, \"This is the day that the Lord has made.\" It was like nothing else I'd ever done. So when I was finished, I asked Jeremy, \"How'd it go?\" He said, \"Great, but you say uh too much.\" I said, \"Well, what can I do about that?\" He said, \"You cannot say it.\” — Andrew Klavan 4:47 The host recalls arriving for the first Daily Wire podcast in bedroom slippers and worrying about his repeated “uh” sounds. Jeremy’s literal solution—simply not saying “uh”—is funny because it is both obvious and unexpectedly effective.
  2. “I can well remember when I brought home my our first child, my daughter, the sudden shock of realizing that this baby was not going to survive uh if my wife didn't take care of it because I'm completely useless. But even I thought I might have some responsibility for taking care of a a child, an actual human being that I had helped create. So I've heard.” — Andrew Klavan 8:36 The host jokes about his helplessness as a new father, saying the baby would not survive without his wife’s care, then adds the sarcastic qualification “So I've heard” to his claim that he helped create the child.
  3. “They tell us to drink, you know, 50 glasses of water every hour. So you spend your entire life in the bathroom.” — Andrew Klavan 1:11:01 Continuing his mockery of changing health advice, Andrew imagines an absurd recommendation to drink fifty glasses of water every hour, leaving someone permanently in the bathroom.
  4. “Now, this I think you can take seriously because everything I do is just so terrific.” — Andrew Klavan 7:09 While discussing his forthcoming book after saying another film should not be taken totally seriously, the host mock-boasts that all his own work is “just so terrific.”

Nothing matches that.
