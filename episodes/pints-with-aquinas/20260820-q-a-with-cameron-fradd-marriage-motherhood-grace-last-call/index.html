Q&A with Cameron Fradd: Marriage, Motherhood & Grace | Last Call Ep. 28

Pints With Aquinas · 20 August 2026 · 31 min · Reached back for, from the archive · written up 26 September at 03:34

Marriage becomes real when comfort, certainty, and convenience give way to patience, sacrifice, and shared struggle.

  • Cameron Fradd says new spouses should put each other’s needs first because marriage ends a self-directed life. 4:43
  • Early marriage was harder without practical help or nearby friends, especially while raising a colicky baby in rural Donegal. 5:46
  • Community later made parenting easier by giving them friends who understood how difficult that season was. 7:56
  • Cameron felt sure they belonged together because her partner saw a better part of her that others missed. 10:09
  • Motherhood shocked her with a difficult pregnancy, hospitalization, C-section, and painful breastfeeding. 13:55
  • Parents can protect family presence by removing distractions and learning their children’s interests alongside them. 21:22

“We lived in the middle of nowhere with zero friends and that was hard.” [5:46]

Worth your time if you want honest stories about marriage, motherhood, doubt, and making family life joyful.
