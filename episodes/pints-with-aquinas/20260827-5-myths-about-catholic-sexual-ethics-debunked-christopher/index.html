The church’s sexual rules make sense only when sex is treated as a language of self-giving love.

  • The church says sex matters because the body is meant to reveal divine love, not because it wants to invade private life. 2:04
  • Natural family planning differs from contraception because it avoids fertile times without deliberately blocking the body’s life-giving power. 4:59
  • Modern natural family planning can identify fertile days with “99 plus percent accuracy,” while conception is possible for only about 24 to 48 hours. 5:27
