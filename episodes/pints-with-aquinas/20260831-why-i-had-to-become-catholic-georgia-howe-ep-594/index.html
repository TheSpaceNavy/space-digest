Why I Had to Become Catholic (Georgia Howe) | Ep. 594

Pints With Aquinas · 31 August 2026 · 1h 57m · Reached back for, from the archive · written up 12 September at 07:14

A viral political break led an emergency nurse through free will, resurrection, science, and finally Catholicism.

  • The guest’s political shift began during lockdown while she worked in a Los Angeles emergency department and watched others stay silent. 5:22
  • Friends urged her to delete a political video as its audience jumped from 100,000 views to more than a million. 9:39
  • A philosophy course made her question determinism—the idea that free will is impossible—because it seemed to undermine moral responsibility. 31:33
  • Tim Keller’s challenge to question her doubts, followed by church experiences, moved Christianity from an idea to a reality. 39:35
  • Catholicism won her over because every Christian depends on authority, while “free-range Christianity” could not reliably identify the true church. 1:11:29
  • Mass, confession, and parish life now feel like practical guardrails and a lifelong homecoming rather than burdens. 1:40:13

“I remember listening to that and thinking, you know, I'm on the wrong train.” [32:01]

Worth your time if you enjoy conversion stories that connect politics, philosophy, science, and ordinary parish life.
