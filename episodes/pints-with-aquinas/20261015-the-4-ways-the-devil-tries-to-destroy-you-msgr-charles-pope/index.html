The 4 Ways the Devil Tries to Destroy You | Msgr. Charles Pope | Last Call Ep. 36

Pints With Aquinas · 15 October 2026 · 35 min · written up 15 October at 15:58

The devil’s most effective attacks may look like truth, political outrage, self-doubt, or even religious devotion.

  • The episode names four tactics: deception, division, diversion, and discouragement. 1:34
  • Deception twists partial truths into extreme falsehoods that pull people away from God. 2:39
  • Separating sex, marriage, and children is blamed for promiscuity, distorted sexuality, and fewer marriages. 4:57
  • Division turns groups against each other through tribal thinking and labels such as oppressor and oppressed. 7:11
  • The speakers urge people to turn outrage over distant events into prayer and practical action nearby. 18:22
  • Discouragement isolates people from God, while humility means accepting real gifts as gifts rather than self-made achievements. 22:20

“Words cannot cook rice.” [18:22]

Worth your time if you want to recognize spiritual attacks hiding inside politics, perfectionism, and everyday frustration.

Most surprising
