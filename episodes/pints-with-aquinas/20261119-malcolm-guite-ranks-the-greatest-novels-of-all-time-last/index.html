Malcolm Guite Ranks the Greatest Novels of All Time | Last Call Ep. 41

Pints With Aquinas · 19 November 2026 · 33 min · written up 20 November at 01:46

Great books do more than entertain: they train the imagination to see faith, evil, desire, and freedom differently.

  • Malcolm Guite gives *The Great Divorce* an S because its images clarify how desire can either control us or draw us toward God. 3:22
  • He praises *The Lord of the Rings* as an epic about letting go, warning that anything we possess can begin to possess us. 6:02
  • Guite ranks *The Once and Future King* below the top tier because its irony weakens the medieval ideal it should present. 7:45
  • He says *The Chronicles of Narnia* may awaken the imagination for Christ more effectively than Tolkien’s deeper literary work. 9:57
  • Guite calls *The Silmarillion* an A, but gives its creation story an S for showing evil as discord within God’s music. 10:59
  • For older or discouraged readers, he recommends starting small with short stories, essays, novellas, Chesterton, Lewis, or Tolkien. 25:33

“Stop possessing it, and it will stop possessing you.” Malcolm Guite [6:02]

Worth your time if you want a friendly guide to finding meaning in the books you missed.

Most surprising
