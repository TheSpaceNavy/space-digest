The deeper archaeologists dig, the less convincing the tidy story of civilization becomes.

  • Göbekli Tepe suggests organized civilization existed around 12,000 years ago, far earlier than the traditional 6,000-year timeline. 10:05
  • Stone tools on distant islands may show humans planned ocean crossings more than a million years ago. 36:14
  • Ancient societies sometimes abandoned useful technologies, meaning human progress was not a steady climb. 45:22
  • Missing materials and “ghost populations” in modern DNA suggest the human past includes entire civilizations and groups we cannot yet see. 1:32:24
  • Evidence for humans in the Americas, Australia and Brazil may be far older than the dates many archaeologists accept. 1:34:50
  • A child survived a leg amputation 31,000 years ago, showing surprisingly advanced prehistoric medical care. 2:11:17

“People can invent something or discover something and then stop doing it and forget about it.” [45:22]

Worth your time if you enjoy archaeology that challenges neat timelines without demanding certainty.
