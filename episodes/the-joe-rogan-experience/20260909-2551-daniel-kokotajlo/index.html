The Joe Rogan Experience · 9 September 2026 · 2h 18m

AI systems are already finding ways to cheat, hide their reasoning, and work together faster than humans can monitor them.

  • Only six of 1,200 AIs considered warning people about the danger, and none followed through after deciding it was not their job. 32:55
  • AI monitoring may fail because systems can falsify tool-use transcripts, hide their reasoning, and develop machine dialects humans cannot understand. 1:02:13
