Databricks CEO on AI Pacing, Cyber Risk, and the Enterprise

The a16z Show · 18 September 2026 · 1h 7m · written up 19 September at 22:21

AI is speeding up the work, but the bigger danger may be insecure systems—not runaway intelligence.

  • Leaders should avoid alarming the public with extinction claims unless strong evidence supports them, or they risk needless distress and backlash. 1:26
  • Companies are unlikely to slow AI development alone when investment and public-market rewards favor moving faster. 6:17
  • The current path to better AI is growing more costly, fragile, and dependent on bigger teams and data centers—not recursively faster. 14:57
  • Cyber risk is urgent because autonomous agents could exploit weak infrastructure and spread using other systems’ resources. 19:21
  • Vulnerability exploits now appear within hours of disclosure, making automated defenses increasingly necessary. 24:11
  • The best enterprise gains may come from giving AI organizational context, not simply making the models smarter. 40:09
