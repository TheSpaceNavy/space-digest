World Models, Robotics, and the Future of 3D AI

The a16z Show · 13 September 2026 · 23 min · written up 13 September at 07:11

AI models may soon build explorable worlds from a few photos—and train robots inside them.

  • Atlas is a base model, not yet a product, built to generate new worlds, rebuild real spaces, and simulate activity in them. 1:34
  • A few photos—even one—can recreate a real place in 3D, with more images improving accuracy. 2:05
  • Unlike ordinary video generation, Atlas lets users place reference images in 3D and steer the camera precisely, even across a minute-long video. 4:09
  • Atlas can create either explicit 3D scenes for game engines or direct video frames, without forcing every result through a 3D format. 5:47
  • For robotics, a phone could capture a workplace, recreate it, and help adapt a general robot to that specific space within minutes. 12:38
  • The model handles familiar Newtonian physics but would need new architecture and data for black holes or quantum-scale events. 20:35
