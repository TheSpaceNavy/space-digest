AI’s biggest opportunities may still be ahead: most businesses barely use it, while robotics could outgrow language models.

  • AI spending is already a larger share of the economy than railroad construction, and the infrastructure build-out could grow tenfold over time. 2:13
  • Most AI revenue comes from about 30 million coders—especially the top million—even though 1.5 billion people do knowledge work. 2:44
  • Enterprise adoption is still below 5%, while shortages across the data-center supply chain may limit computing capacity until at least 2028. 4:32
  • Frontier labs may build broad tools and coding products, but specialized companies can still win by handling industry rules, integrations, and workflows. 13:52
  • Most consumers use AI like a search engine today, while children are already using it to create music and other original work. 21:35
  • Robotics may become a larger market than language AI, starting with factory tasks before driving a major productivity boom. 31:34
