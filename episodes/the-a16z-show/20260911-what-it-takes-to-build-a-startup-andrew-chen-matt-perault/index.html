What It Takes to Build a Startup | Andrew Chen & Matt Perault

The a16z Show · 11 September 2026 · 38 min · written up 11 September at 06:20

Tiny startups now move faster than the systems meant to regulate them—and many founders start with no fixed idea.

  • Speedrun invests up to $1 million in brand-new startups and supports them through a 12-week program before outside fundraising. 1:35
  • It backs unusually capable people more than fixed ideas, because early concepts often change sharply. 2:38
  • The program focuses on “little tech”: two- or three-person teams that may work from kitchen tables or bootstrap first. 4:47
