Nas, Grandmaster Caz, Steve Stoute & Ben Horowitz on Paying Hip-Hop’s Pioneers Their Due

The a16z Show · 20 September 2026 · 57 min · written up 20 September at 22:41

Hip-hop’s pioneers were not just overlooked—they were sometimes one medical emergency away from disaster.

  • The Paid in Full Foundation combines financial help with public recognition because money alone would not earn hip-hop pioneers’ trust. 3:12
  • Grandmaster Kaz’s rhymes helped create “Rapper’s Delight,” yet he received neither proper credit nor payment. 9:00
  • The foundation’s events rebuild connections between generations, including Dr. Dre asking to meet Kool Moe Dee and Slick Rick. 15:43
