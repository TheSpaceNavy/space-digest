Building a Team at AI Speed | Harvey’s Maggie Landers

The a16z Show · 27 September 2026 · 48 min · written up 27 September at 17:47

Harvey is growing at AI speed without giving up autonomy, openness, or the pressure to move unusually fast.

  • Harvey grew by more than 1,000 employees after Maggie joined and expected to reach about 2,000 by year’s end. 3:19
  • The company values quick experiments and learning from failure more than academic perfection. 7:45
  • Its demanding pace is balanced by teamwork, personal freedom, and fast feedback on people’s work. 9:18
  • Founders stay involved in both major strategy and small details while remaining easy to reach. 12:30
  • Employees can read founder memos, board decks, and internal question-and-answer sessions. 16:17
  • Harvey seeks people who can handle uncertainty and build missing processes instead of waiting for order. 43:52

“You’re going to do more in a month at Harvey than you would in a quarter somewhere else.” Maggie Landers [7:11]
