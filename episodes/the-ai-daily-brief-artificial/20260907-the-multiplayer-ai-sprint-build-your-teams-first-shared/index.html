The Multiplayer AI Sprint: Build Your Team’s First Shared Agent

The AI Daily Brief · 7 September 2026 · 22 min

AI agents are moving from private helpers to shared teammates that can work inside the spaces teams already use.

  • Most knowledge work happens with others, yet most AI agents still serve one person alone. 3:24
  • Anthropic says 65% of its product team’s code now comes from a shared Claude agent working across the team. 8:43
  • Open Claw’s maintainers found that sharing the live agent session mattered more than merely seeing another user online. 10:10
  • The proposed four-part team sprint covers an AI-use inventory, shared context, overlapping work, and testing one shared agent. 13:33
  • Teams should choose shared-agent projects by scoring common need, information drift, restricted data, and how easily results can be checked. 18:21

“The session stops being a private conversation between one developer and a model.” [10:10]
