How to Build an AI-Native Company Today

The AI Daily Brief · 6 September 2026 · 24 min · Reached back for · written up 10 September at 19:23

AI-native companies will not just add AI to old work; they will rebuild how work gets done.

  • Companies should map current work to capture hidden knowledge, but avoid forcing agents to copy outdated human workflows. 3:19
  • Every employee needs a work environment for using models, tools, context, and code—not just access to ChatGPT or Claude. 4:47
  • Organizations should treat change as constant, repeatedly redesigning systems instead of protecting workflows built only months earlier. 7:42
  • Non-engineers will build useful tools, while engineers provide governance and shared standards so those tools can reach production safely. 13:32
  • AI-native companies will test hundreds or thousands of marketing variations before spending heavily, while human taste still guides major brand work. 15:56
  • Agents should earn independence step by step, moving from observation to suggestions, approved actions, and finally bounded autonomy. 22:15

“Autonomy must be earned.” [22:15]
