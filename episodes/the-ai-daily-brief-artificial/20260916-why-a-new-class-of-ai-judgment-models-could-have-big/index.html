Why a New Class of AI “Judgment Models” Could Have Big Business Implications

The AI Daily Brief · 16 September 2026 · 25 min · written up 18 September at 09:57

A cheap new kind of AI could make software judge messy human situations at massive scale.

  • JEV does not write text; it answers narrow questions with probabilities, such as whether a customer sounds angry. 12:41
  • TypeSafe says its models are trained for honest confidence levels rather than answers people prefer reading. 13:15
  • The model could work beside language models: one suggests options, JEV judges them, and software takes action. 17:27
  • In a test, JEV returned 777 judgments on 37 documents in under 0.7 seconds for about a quarter of a cent. 19:03
