#338 Cardinal Burke - The New Testament

The Shawn Ryan Show · 10 September 2026 · 2h 43m · written up 12 September at 07:06

The episode argues that Christianity rests on a physical Resurrection and demands a radically different way of living.

  • The Resurrection is defended through the empty tomb, Paul’s conversion, and apostles who changed from fearful followers into martyrs. 2:56
  • The Gospels were written less than a century after Jesus’s death, drawing on memorized oral accounts. 10:08
  • Mary is presented as preserved from sin and as someone who always directs believers toward Jesus. 44:10
  • Catholics believe the Eucharist is literally Christ’s body and blood, not merely a symbol. 1:31:00
  • The apostles initially fled and hid, but later preached boldly despite persecution and death. 1:52:46
  • The speaker says fathers must discipline and protect children rather than simply try to be their friends. 2:16:30

“All of them were martyrs except St. John.” — Cardinal Burke [8:42]

Worth your time if you want a direct, traditional explanation of Christianity’s central claims.

Most surprising

  1. “They were written less than a century after our Lord died.” 10:08A reader might expect the Gospel accounts to have been recorded much later.
  2. Mary was preserved from every stain of sin from the moment of her conception. 45:34Readers might expect Mary’s holiness to begin later, through her choices.
  3. “Today you will be with me in paradise.” 1:42:01The condemned thief receives an immediate promise of salvation despite his criminal past.
  4. The speaker says demonic activity is occurring “a lot” in the present time, including sometimes within the Church. 2:21:48Readers may expect demons to be treated mainly as historical or symbolic concerns.
  5. The image of Our Lady of Guadalupe remains on a cactus-cloth garment that should have disintegrated within 30 or 40 years. 2:37:00An extremely fragile garment would normally be expected to decay centuries ago.
