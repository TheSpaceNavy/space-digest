318: Prudence, Fast and Slow

The OptimalWork Podcast · 21 September 2026 · 32 min · written up 21 September at 23:10

Your first instinct can help—but only if you treat it as a question, not an answer.

  • Counsel keeps prudence grounded in reality by testing whether your mental picture matches what is actually happening. 1:59
  • Curiosity protects against overfamiliarity by keeping you interested in the real person or situation rather than your fixed model. 2:57
  • Fast intuition is useful when shaped by experience, but it should launch deeper investigation instead of ending it. 7:41
  • Fear and desire can make people accept distorted first impressions as reality, cutting off careful thought. 15:52
  • Good counsel investigates the present situation, remembers similar cases, and asks others what worked for them. 19:40
  • A powerful check before a major decision is to imagine it failed disastrously a year from now and ask how it went wrong. 29:43

“Curiosity is the driving force of counsel.” — Kevin Major [30:39]

Worth your time if you want better decisions without blindly trusting either instinct or analysis.
