Replay: Principles and Algorithms for Work and Life | Ray Dalio (2019)

The a16z Show · 25 November 2026 · 50 min · written up 25 November at 15:38

Better decisions come from testing your beliefs, knowing your limits, and spreading risk—not from being right alone.

  • Ray defines principles as reusable rules that turn past experience into better decisions. 2:59
  • History reveals patterns that repeat, while showing which parts of the present are truly different. 6:58
  • Investment strategies should be built like engineering projects: test indicators, study failures, and improve them. 11:32
  • Black-box systems are risky when nobody can explain why they made a decision or whether the past still applies. 14:30
  • Computers are most useful as partners that challenge human judgment and highlight disagreements. 17:52
  • Diversifying across about 15 low-correlation investments can cut risk more effectively than choosing only the “best” bets. 21:11
  • Strong teams openly map people’s strengths and weaknesses, then use those differences to cover individual gaps. 25:04
  • Good decisions give more weight to people with proven expertise instead of treating every opinion equally. 33:56
