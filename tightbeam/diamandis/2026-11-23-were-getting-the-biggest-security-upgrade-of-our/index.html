We're Getting the Biggest Security Upgrade of Our Lifetime

Monday 23 November 2026 · 1299 words · about 6 min read

AI may uncover a wave of hidden software flaws, but it could also help defenders repair them faster than ever.

Peter Diamandis expects AI to expose many weaknesses in old software over the next few years, possibly causing frightening attacks and outages. He also predicts that AI could patch almost every known vulnerability within two years.

He believes defenders may have an early advantage because leading AI labs can give security teams access before the tools become widely available. But repairs still depend on maintainers having enough authority, money, time, and systems for recovery when prevention fails.
