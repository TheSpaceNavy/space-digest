What Counts as a Moat in the Age of AI?

Thursday 24 September 2026 · 1762 words · about 8 min read

AI is erasing traditional business moats, leaving reinvention speed, capital, and selective brand strength as the main defenses.

Patents, proprietary code, distribution, switching costs, and other classic advantages depended on copying being slow and expensive. AI can now reproduce software, adapt code to different chips, and weaken customer ties to storefronts.

The remaining defenses are moving faster than rivals, deploying large amounts of capital, and—in limited cases—owning a powerful luxury brand. The piece urges leaders to treat existing advantages as temporary head starts and build the ability to create new ones.
