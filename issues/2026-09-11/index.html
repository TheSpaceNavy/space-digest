Your AI Doctor Is Coming | Julie Yoo

The a16z Show · 6 September 2026 · 27 min · Reached back for · written up 11 September at 06:20

Healthcare’s slow start with technology may let it leap straight into an AI-powered system built around patients.

  • Healthcare went from paper files to electronic records when the government paid doctors tens of thousands of dollars to adopt them. 1:36
  • Julie Yoo’s company tackled long waits by matching patients with unused appointment capacity, exposing a major gap between demand and doctors’ time. 3:11
  • COVID forced changes to doctor licensing and payment rules, while AI made digital healthcare easier to build and adopt. 5:55
  • Healthcare may leap over older workplace software because it never spent heavily on the layers other industries now have to replace. 13:50
  • Doctors are adopting AI scribes on their own because the tools work, while patients use chatbots to avoid months-long waits or costly emergency rooms. 14:56
  • The next step is moving from answers to lasting care: testing, treatment, referrals, and an ongoing relationship with an AI-supported doctor. 18:37

“It's a 15-year overnight success.” [5:21]

Worth your time if you want to understand where AI could make healthcare cheaper, faster, and more personal.
