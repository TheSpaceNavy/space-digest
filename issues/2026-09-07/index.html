You Are Not Data (Christopher West) on AI and the Soul | Ep. 595

Pints With Aquinas · 7 September 2026 · 2h 40m · written up 7 September at 20:57

AI may be powerful, but the episode argues that treating humans like machines destroys what makes love, bodies, and relationships human.

  • AI forces a basic question: if machines can perform intellectual tasks, is intelligence enough to define a human being? 5:08
  • Chatbots offer comforting affirmation without rejection or the difficulty of real relationships, giving them an appeal the speakers compare to pornography. 11:40
  • Christianity is presented as salvation of the body, not escape from it through a soul uploaded to the cloud. 40:54
  • The speakers say AI cannot cure loneliness because true communion requires human persons, not machines that imitate them. 51:45
  • They define intelligence as personally finding meaning between alternatives, while AI mainly imitates selected human abilities with greater speed and computation. 1:11:30
  • They warn that when imitation becomes normal, people may accept technology’s substitutes for friendship, love, communication, and physical community. 2:01:04

“When the imitation of humanity becomes the norm of humanity, we've lost our humanity.” [2:01:04]

Worth your time if you wonder whether technology can imitate the human person without replacing real love.

Answering 7 Arguments For Atheism | Joe Schmid | Last Call Ep. 30

Pints With Aquinas · 3 September 2026 · 29 min · written up 7 September at 20:56

The episode argues that suffering, hiddenness, cosmic order, and even religious disagreement can fit belief in God.

  • Suffering may not disprove God because people could be unaware of divine reasons for allowing it. 1:28
  • Pain can make room for forgiveness, courage, character, and meaningful effects on other people. 2:54
  • God’s apparent hiddenness might allow evangelism, future relationship, or an unrecognized relationship with sincere nonbelievers. 4:51
  • Naturalism explains ordinary events but, the speaker argues, struggles with miracles and the universe’s fine-tuned laws and constants. 7:46
  • Religious diversity may create chances to share truth, while culturally shaped beliefs are not automatically false. 9:37
  • Omnipotence means being able to do anything possible, so the stone paradox relies on a logical impossibility. 13:29
