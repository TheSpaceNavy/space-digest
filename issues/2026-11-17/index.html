The a16z Show · 17 November 2026 · 18 min · written up 17 November at 14:37

AI can already speed up AI research, but its biggest weakness is knowing which ideas matter.

  • Recursive self-improvement currently means using AI to make research faster, not having models build new models alone. 2:54
  • AI research “interns” can run scoped experiments and report results, but they still need humans to set the question and direction. 4:01
  • One researcher estimates AI is about 80% along the broader AGI spectrum, but only 25% to 50% along the recursive-research spectrum. 5:02

  • Progress may hit a compute wall unless AI helps design smaller experiments that predict results at larger scales. 10:40
