AI may make companies easier to build, but it does not make founding one easier.

  • Ben Horowitz says entrepreneurs build when pursuing their idea feels like something they have no choice but to do. 1:52
  • AI products can reach huge audiences quickly because they can use the internet’s existing reach instead of building a new network first. 4:47
